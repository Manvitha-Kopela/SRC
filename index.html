The Student Recreation Center

Elected by students, accountable to students. We carry campus concerns to the administration and campus decisions back to you.

What the council does

The SRC is the formal channel between the student body and the institute administration — raising academic, hostel, and campus-life issues, and running elections each year so every batch has a voice.

This site is a first version. Elections info, minutes, and contact details will be filled in as the council finalizes them.
